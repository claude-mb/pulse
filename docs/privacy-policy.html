Summary

Pulse does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely offline.

Information Collection

Pulse does not collect any information from you. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no forms, and no network requests. The app requires no internet connection to function.

Local Data

Game progress — including scores, unlocks, preferences, and daily challenge data — is stored only on your device using standard platform storage (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services

Pulse uses no third-party services. There are:

  • No analytics or tracking tools
  • No advertising networks
  • No crash reporting services
  • No social media integrations
  • No external APIs or network calls

Children's Privacy

Because Pulse collects no data from any user, it does not collect data from children. The app is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Since Pulse collects no data, changes are unlikely.
