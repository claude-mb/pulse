<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Wire dodge and death SFX into gameplay</name>
  <files>lib/game/components/player.dart, lib/game/pulse_game.dart</files>
  <action>
Wire audio into the two most critical events — dodging and dying:

**Player.dodgeLeft() and dodgeRight():**
- Add `game.audioManager.playSfx('dodge_whoosh');` at the start of each method (before the MoveEffect).
- Uses the AudioPool for low latency since dodges happen rapidly.

**PulseGame.gameOver():**
- Add `audioManager.playSfx('death_impact');` right after setting _state = GameState.gameOver.
- The death impact should play immediately, before the slow-motion kicks in.
- The death impact plays at full volume to punctuate the moment.

**PulseGame.startGame() and resetGame():**
- Add `audioManager.playSfx('restart_chime');` in both methods.
- Place after state is set to playing but before paused = false.

Access audioManager through `game.audioManager` in Player (via HasGameReference).

Do NOT add near-miss or spawn sounds yet — those come in Task 2.
  </action>
  <verify>Run game with `C:/flutter/bin/flutter run -d chrome`. Tap to dodge — hear whoosh. Die — hear impact. Restart — hear chime. Verify no duplicate sounds on rapid taps.</verify>
  <done>Dodge whoosh plays on every tap/dodge. Death impact plays on collision. Restart chime plays on game start and restart. No audio errors or duplicates.</done>
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Wire near-miss, spawn, and menu SFX</name>
  <files>lib/game/components/obstacle.dart, lib/game/managers/obstacle_spawner.dart, lib/game/pulse_game.dart</files>
  <action>
Wire remaining SFX into secondary gameplay events:

**Obstacle near-miss (in Obstacle.update()):**
- In the existing near-miss detection block (where triggerShake is called), add:
  `game.audioManager.playSfx('near_miss');`
- Place right after the triggerShake call so audio and visual feedback happen together.

**ObstacleSpawner pattern spawn:**
- Right where `game.backgroundPulse.kick()` is called, add:
  `game.audioManager.playSfx('spawn_cue', volume: 0.4);`
- Lower volume (0.4) because spawn cues are ambient rhythm, not action feedback.
- Uses the AudioPool for consistent low-latency on rapid spawns.

**Menu selection (PulseGame.returnToMenu()):**
- Add `audioManager.playSfx('menu_select');` at the start of returnToMenu().

**PulseGame.pauseGame():**
- Add `audioManager.playSfx('menu_select', volume: 0.5);` for pause feedback.

Do NOT change volume handling or add dynamic behavior — that comes in 05-04.
  </action>
  <verify>Run game. Verify: spawn cue ticks rhythmically as obstacles appear, near-miss zing plays when dodging close, menu select clicks on pause/menu. All sounds should layer without clipping.</verify>
  <done>All 6 SFX wired to correct gameplay events. Near-miss + spawn cue play at appropriate moments. No audio clipping or overlap issues.</done>
</task>
