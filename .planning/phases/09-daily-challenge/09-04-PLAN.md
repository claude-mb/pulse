The DAILY button goes in the center column, between the "BEST X" display and the bottom buttons. Add it after the best score display (after the `if (bestScore > 0)` block), with appropriate spacing.

Implementation:
1. Add a horizontal divider line (thin, 120px wide, GameConfig.textColor at 0.15 alpha) with 24px top spacing.
2. Below divider (8px spacing), add a GestureDetector wrapping a "DAILY CHALLENGE" text button. Style: GameConfig.accentColor, 18px, bold, letterSpacing 3. Use HitTestBehavior.opaque on the GestureDetector. On tap: call `widget.game.startDailyChallenge()`.
3. Below the button (4px spacing), show daily stats in a Row:

CRITICAL: The full-screen GestureDetector that starts endless mode must NOT intercept the DAILY button tap. The DAILY button's GestureDetector with HitTestBehavior.opaque will handle this automatically since it's in a Stack with the full-screen detector — Flutter's hit testing gives priority to more specific widgets (the button) over the full-screen detector beneath.

Actually, looking at the current MainMenu structure: the full-screen GestureDetector wraps the entire Container. The DAILY button needs to be INSIDE the center Column but wrapped in its own GestureDetector that calls startDailyChallenge instead. Since GestureDetectors nested inside another GestureDetector will consume the tap (preventing the parent from firing), this works correctly — tapping DAILY starts daily mode, tapping elsewhere starts endless mode.</action>
